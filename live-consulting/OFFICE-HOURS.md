# AI Office Hours & Live Problem Solving

**Free group office hours:** 30–45 minutes weekly; beginner questions, demonstrations and safe troubleshooting.

**Fix My Workflow LIVE:** 45 minutes weekly or biweekly; volunteer submits a process; define problem, measure baseline, apply Negative Step Zero, demonstrate changes, measure again.

**Private consulting (optional):** 15-minute fit call; paid 30/60-minute sessions after demand validation. Pricing is a test hypothesis, not an established rate. Use a private booking link; never post private meeting URLs or personal data in the community.

## Session checklist
1. Confirm question, outcome, and permissions.
2. Ask participant to redact credentials, customer data and sensitive records.
3. Get explicit recording and reuse consent separately; default to no recording.
4. Diagnose existing workflow before suggesting additional software.
5. Demonstrate with synthetic data when public.
6. Record baseline, outcome, unresolved issues and follow-up steps.
7. Share a sanitized summary only if approved.

## Proposed first live agenda (30 minutes)
0–5 welcome and safety; 5–12 AI basics and tokens; 12–25 audience questions; 25–30 one useful challenge and next event.
