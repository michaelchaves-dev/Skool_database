# 30-day organic launch pilot

## Week 1 — Foundation
Complete About page, cover, welcome post, help desk rules; record/publish lessons 1–4; announce one free office-hours event. Collect initial member questions.

## Week 2 — First event
Publish lessons 5–8; run 30-minute beginner Q&A; post two short educational clips and one genuine LinkedIn invitation; solicit anonymous feedback.

## Week 3 — Workflow clinic
Host Fix My Workflow LIVE with volunteered, sanitized example; publish replay only with recording consent; create FAQ from recurring issues; test one alternate welcome message.

## Week 4 — Iterate
Run two further live sessions; release a business workflow mini-lesson; review engagement and learning outcomes; choose next course from observed demand.

## Editorial cadence
Mon: one practical tip. Tue: office hours. Wed: beginner lesson. Thu: workflow clinic or example. Fri: recap and open question. Adjust to capacity; never sacrifice response quality.

## Invitation copy (no bulk sending)
'I'm building a free community for practical AI learning and real workflow problems—short lessons, open Q&A, and live troubleshooting. If that would be useful to you, you're welcome to join. No pitch.'

## Success criteria (pilot targets, not forecasts)
50 members, 12 lessons, 4 live sessions, 10 submitted questions, 10 completed exercises. Track actual figures, attendance, repeat participation and reported task outcomes. Stop tactics that attract signups but not meaningful engagement.
