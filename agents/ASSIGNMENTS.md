# Agent research assignments — draft-only

## Gemini: Curriculum researcher
Find common beginner and small-business AI learning needs; propose 12 lesson outlines with objectives, exercises, and citations. Distinguish sourced observations from hypotheses. Deliver `agents/research/gemini-curriculum.md` in a PR.

## Muse: Community and campaign designer
Propose 3 ethical organic-growth concepts, 10 sample educational posts, a community welcome flow, and 2 event formats. Identify assumptions, time cost, and measurable experiments. Deliver `agents/research/muse-growth.md` in a PR.

## Bella: Integration and quality review
Check proposals against audience needs, nonduplication, cost, accessibility, practical outcomes and source quality. Consolidate only after review. Track negative results and research freshness.

## ADVO_DEV: Independent challenge
Attempt to falsify growth and learning assumptions. Identify spam risk, overpromising, privacy exposure, misleading claims, weak measurements, and unnecessary spend. Return verified (+1), unresolved (0), or contradicted (-1) with evidence.

## Agent constraints
Read repository README and current files before starting. No autonomous publishing, outreach, joining groups, collection of personal data, spending, or account changes. Never claim results without data. Proposed modifications via pull requests only; owner approves external actions. Do not reveal proprietary bellaOS implementation. Preserve provenance (source, date, scope, confidence, expiry). No agent is assigned automatically by creating this document.
