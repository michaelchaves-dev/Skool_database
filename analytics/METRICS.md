# Weekly pilot scorecard

Record each week: date range; members at start/end; new members; active posters/commenters; submitted questions; first responses; lesson starts/completions; event registrations/attendance; consulting inquiries/bookings; time spent; direct cost; user-reported time saved with method; complaints and opt-outs.

## Experiment log template
Hypothesis | baseline | intervention | time spent | cost | observations | source/evidence | verdict (-1/0/+1) | next step | review date.

Avoid equating likes or follower count with learning. Record zero-result searches and expire/recheck when sources change. Do not store sensitive participant details or raw meeting recordings in this public repository.
